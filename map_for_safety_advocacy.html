WHY THIS MATTERS

Maps can help protect lives.

Geodetic Engineering connects accurate positioning, measurement, mapping, and spatial data. These tools help communities understand where things are and make better-informed safety plans.

📍

Know the Location

Accurate coordinates and map features help identify homes, roads, schools, clinics, and evacuation areas.

⚠️

Understand the Risk

Hazard information can be mapped to show places that may be exposed to flooding, landslides, or other dangers.

🧭

Plan Safer Movement

Route maps can help people locate possible paths to safety when verified and used with official instructions.

BE PREPARED

Make safety a community habit.

Preparedness starts before an emergency happens. A little knowledge can make a big difference.

Prepare

Know your area's official evacuation sites and keep emergency contact details accessible.

Check

Use updated information from your local government and trusted disaster-management authorities.

Share

Help family and neighbors understand safe locations and emergency procedures.

OUR ADVOCACY

Map for Safety. Prepare for Tomorrow.

We advocate the responsible use of maps and geospatial information to build safer, more informed communities. A map is more than a picture of a place—it can be a tool for awareness, preparedness, and protecting lives.
